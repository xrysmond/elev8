First takahē chick hatches at a restored New Zealand site

Eight threatened birds were released at Pūponga on Onetahua/Farewell Spit only months ago. Now the first chick has hatched.

The parents formed a new pair after arriving, while years of habitat restoration, predator control and a four-kilometre fence helped prepare the site. The chick’s survival is not assured, and teams say the next few months remain important.

Source: New Zealand Department of Conservation. Reported Nov 18, 2026.

Side hustle idea

Prepare holiday card address lists and mailing packs

The late-November mailing window creates a short, practical opportunity to help busy households turn an approved contact list into an organized, stamp-ready holiday-card bundle.

$45–150Startup cost
18 hrsDelivery a month, example

Example: 6 jobs × $95 = $570 a month; about $438 after $132 estimated costs, before taxes and owner pay. An illustration, not guaranteed earnings. Starting income may be $0.

The market
202520262033 proj.

Global greeting-cards market, per Grand View Research. North America is 42.7% of revenue; projected growth is 1.9% a year, 2026 to 2033. Industry estimates, not personal earnings.

The details

Clients pay for approved-list cleanup, label printing and a stamp-ready bundle. Cards, postage and final mailing authorization remain theirs.

The report covers the global greeting-cards sector across North America, Europe, Asia Pacific, Central and South America, and the Middle East and Africa. Seasonal occasions remain an important demand driver.

This is industry revenue, not an estimate of what one local organizer can earn.

The worldwide greeting-cards industry is projected to grow at a 1.9% CAGR from 2026 through 2033. This market forecast does not predict individual service income.

Estimate USD 45–150 for labels, pens, scissors and secure storage, excluding printer, postage, insurance, registration and taxes.

Ask ten local households which holiday-card task they would outsource, then seek two paid pilots with written scope and deletion terms.

Treat address lists as private data, get final written approval, delete files on schedule and check local business, tax and insurance rules.
